# Project Vision Statement

## Project Name
**IntelliTrack: AI-Powered Internship Management System**

---

## Purpose

IntelliTrack aims to provide Bo's Coffee with a centralized internship management platform that streamlines attendance monitoring, daily work log submission, performance evaluation, internship progress tracking, and AI-assisted portfolio generation to improve the overall internship management process.

---

## Target Users

IntelliTrack is designed for four primary user groups:

### 1. Interns (Students)
Interns use the system to record attendance, submit daily work logs, monitor internship progress, receive supervisor evaluations, participate in workmate feedback, manage internship documents, and generate AI-assisted professional internship portfolios.

### 2. Company Supervisors
Company Supervisors oversee assigned interns by monitoring attendance, reviewing daily work logs, assigning tasks, conducting performance evaluations, verifying attendance records, requesting workmate feedback, and tracking intern development throughout the internship.

### 3. Company Representatives
Company Representatives (e.g., HR Personnel, Internship Coordinators, or Training Officers) manage internship operations within the organization by registering interns, assigning supervisors, maintaining company information, monitoring internship activities, and generating company-level reports.

### 4. School Coordinators
School Coordinators monitor internship progress across partner companies by reviewing attendance records, rendered hours, supervisor evaluations, internship completion status, and generating reports to ensure students satisfy academic internship requirements.

---

## Value Proposition

IntelliTrack provides Bo's Coffee with a centralized internship management platform that simplifies attendance monitoring, work log submission, performance evaluation, and internship tracking while using Artificial Intelligence (AI) to analyze intern progress and automatically generate professional internship portfolios.

## Future Vision

IntelliTrack envisions becoming a reliable internship management platform that enhances collaboration between interns, company supervisors, company representatives, and school coordinators through centralized monitoring, AI-assisted performance analysis, and automated portfolio generation. The system aims to improve the efficiency, transparency, and overall internship experience while serving as a practical solution for managing internship programs within participating organizations.

---

## Vision Summary

IntelliTrack seeks to modernize internship management by providing an integrated platform that enables efficient attendance monitoring, internship tracking, performance evaluation, and AI-assisted portfolio generation. By centralizing internship-related processes into a single system, IntelliTrack supports effective collaboration among educational institutions, partner companies, and interns while promoting transparency, accountability, and professional development throughout the internship journey.
